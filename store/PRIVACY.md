# Runstart 0-GOAL – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Runstart 0-GOAL ("the app") is made by Jesper Laursen / Team Ingberg. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: **the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.**

## What is stored, and where
- Your training programme, workout history, distances, milestones and settings are stored **only on your own phone**.
- There is no account, no login and no server. The developer cannot see your data.
- If you delete the app, or use "Delete all data" in Settings, the data is removed.

## Location (GPS)
- The app asks for access to your location only when you choose an **outdoor** run, to measure distance, pace and speed.
- Location is used while a run is in progress (also with the screen locked) and is **never sent anywhere**. GPS points are kept on the phone only while the run is in progress (so an interrupted run can be resumed) and are deleted when it ends – only the resulting distance and time are saved.
- On a treadmill the app does not use GPS at all.

## Notifications
- Training reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent over the internet.

## Backup
- If you choose "Export backup", the app creates a file and opens the system share sheet. You decide where the file goes. The app never uploads it by itself.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking
- The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children
- The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/Caritas-dk/Runstart/issues or see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

# Runstart 0-GOAL – Privatlivspolitik (dansk)

*Senest opdateret: 22. november 2026*

Runstart 0-GOAL ("appen") er lavet af Jesper Laursen / Team Ingberg. Kort fortalt: **appen indsamler, sender og deler ingen personlige data.**

- **Lagring:** Træningsprogram, historik, distancer, milepæle og indstillinger gemmes kun på din egen telefon. Der er ingen konto, intet login og ingen server.
- **GPS:** Appen beder kun om adgang til din placering, når du vælger et udendørs løbepas, for at måle distance, tempo og hastighed. Placeringen bruges kun under løbepasset (også med skærmen låst), sendes aldrig nogen steder hen. GPS-punkterne gemmes kun midlertidigt under løbepasset og slettes bagefter – kun distance og tid gemmes. På løbebånd bruges GPS slet ikke.
- **Påmindelser:** Lokale notifikationer på telefonen. Intet sendes over internettet.
- **Backup:** Du bestemmer selv, hvor en eksporteret backupfil sendes hen. Appen uploader den aldrig selv.
- **Ingen analyse, reklamer eller sporing.**
- **Kontakt:** https://github.com/Caritas-dk/Runstart/issues
